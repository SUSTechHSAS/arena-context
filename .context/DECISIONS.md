- Owner-directed: Rust; three-dimensional torus physics, climate/ecology, configurable planet scale, on-demand refinement to the requested 1 m³ unit. Full wording: OWNER_REQUEST.md.
- Workflow: plan first, then implement the human-reviewed plan. Imported specifications are reference inputs, not verified results.
- Deferred payload remains opaque until final acceptance and closure of the complete terrain-generation task.
- Plan review (session arena/d97b3c57-arena-context → arena/f7309931-arena-context): reference plan adopted as blueprint with 12 audit findings (F1–F12); strict 1 m³ surfaced as decision D1; machine budgets became machine-relative; unified acceptance matrix with GATE/RAIL classification; 7 open decisions for Kibiandkimi.
- Prioritized decision ordering: D1 (voxel) and D2 (planet size) are P0 blockers; D3 (machine) and D5 (disk) are P1; D4/D6/D7 are P2 and can wait until M0 is running.
- 2026-10-07 (plan-review, session arena/d97b3c57-arena-context): Adopted the TerraGen7 reference plan (v1.1) as the implementation blueprint after a full audit (docs/plan/00-audit.md): 15 independent numeric spot checks passed; the plan is internally consistent.
- Strict 1 m³ vs stretched voxels: the reference's nominal 1 m x-spacing (physical width k(σ) ∈ [0.75, 1.25] m) does NOT satisfy a strict reading of "1 m³ minimum unit". Surfaced as owner decision D1 with options A/B/C (recommendation: C — regular internal grid, export path resampled to exact 1 m³). Not silently relaxed, per TASK.md.
- Torus physics feasibility: SCF equilibrium of a self-gravitating rotating torus is literature-supported; added feasibility gate G1 (convergence, Ω²>0, no mass loss through the ring hole, two-grid Ω agreement) with a documented fallback (adjust R/r or Ω, or rigid mode with geoid-deviation warning). Physical assumptions made explicit as PA-1..PA-12.
- Machine budget: the reference budget (Ryzen 7 5700U 8C/16T, 13 GB RAM, 4.3 GB free) is a record of the old local environment and does not apply here. Current sandbox measured 2026-10-07: 2 vCPU Xeon 2.60 GHz, 3.8 GB RAM, 20 GB free. All time budgets are now machine-relative (per-core cell-steps/s calibrated by `terragen bench`); absolute memory/disk budgets retained and split between dev/CI machine and target machine.
- Storage: the reference's "world dir ≤ 1.5 GB + keep 2 checkpoints + full field set" is self-inconsistent at the earth preset (~1.92 GB by our accounting). Per-field storage budget table added (docs/plan/01 §5); cap decision (2.0 GB vs 1.5 GB with 1 checkpoint) is owner decision D5.
- Acceptance: module-level T-tests consolidated into a single verifiable acceptance matrix (docs/plan/02-acceptance-matrix.md) with measurement methods, thresholds, commands, evidence, and GATE/RAIL classification.
- Review workflow: the reference's model assignment (Opus/Sonnet/Haiku) and local git workflow do not apply; replaced by the arena-context Arena protocol (SUSTechHSAS submits, Kibiandkimi reviews, work/1/<unit> branches, PRs to AerraGen-main, no auto-merge). Milestone gates M0–M8 and review severity levels retained.
- GCM (highest-risk item) fallback formalized as decision gate D7: two failed rounds → Gill-type linear wind model + EBM temperature, recorded in report and CHANGELOG.
- Determinism rules extended to the export path (same region exported twice, 1 vs 16 threads, byte-identical).
